## 3. Themes and why the titles work

Sample is 3–4 posts, so treat this as a pattern read, not a finding.

- **Safety / crime dominates what got picked up** (2 of 3 attributable lists, 3 of 4 with the unattributed one). Money/spending is the other theme (admin spend).
- **Every pickup was a named school reacting to its own rank.** West Chester's local newsletter, Macalester's student paper, Missouri State's safety office. The list spreads through the schools on it, not through general interest. INFERRED mechanic: a logo on a "bad" list gives students, alumni and rivals a reason to send it to each other.
- **Title formula:** number ("Top 20") + superlative ("highest", "most") + a negative or embarrassing metric. All verified titles follow it.
- **Official-sounding source footer** (Clery Act / Dept of Education / FBI) lends authority and makes the post hard to wave away.
- **Rivalry is built in:** a 20-row list with logos puts conference rivals side by side.
- **The weak spot Leland can use:** both press pieces attack the method. Mac Weekly: raw Clery counts reward schools where reporting is easier, so a high rank can mean a better reporting culture. KY3: citywide crime shown under a university logo. Neither list was shown to be normalised properly. A rebuilt version with stated denominators and caveats is defensible where theirs was not.

Data sources seen or implied: Clery Act / Campus Safety and Security data (twice, VERIFIED), FBI crime data (unattributed post), IPEDS Finance (INFERRED for admin spend). No evidence found of College Scorecard or Section 117 foreign-gift use; that would need the feed itself.

## 4. Chart forms

### A. The four lists found

| List | Better chart form | Why | Dataset to rebuild with verified numbers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admin spend per student | Slope chart between two fiscal years (or a bump chart across several), with one ranked-bar slide for the latest year | A single-year rank hides who is climbing; change over time is the story | IPEDS Finance (institutional support) + 12-month FTE enrollment, https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/use-the-data |
| Sexual assault rates (Clery) | Dot plot of rate per 1,000 students, with raw count shown as a second, smaller mark | Separates big schools from high-rate schools and exposes the small-denominator problem behind Macalester's 6th place | Campus Safety and Security Data Analysis Cutting Tool, https://ope.ed.gov/campussafety/ ; enrollment from IPEDS |
| Liquor-law arrests | Scatter: arrests vs. disciplinary referrals per 1,000 students | Schools choose to arrest or refer; the scatter shows enforcement style, which the list hides | Same Clery tool (arrests and referrals tables) |
| Dangerous college towns | Two-column dot plot: city violent-crime rate next to on-campus Clery rate for the same school | Answers the exact objection Missouri State raised (city vs campus) | FBI Crime Data Explorer https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/ + Clery tool |
