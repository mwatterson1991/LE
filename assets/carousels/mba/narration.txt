Slide 1
What does it take to get an MBA? Here are the numbers at seven schools: Harvard, Stanford, Wharton, Kellogg, Chicago Booth, Columbia and MIT Sloan.

Slide 2
The latest classes scored between 675 and 695 on the GMAT Focus. Stanford reports an average of 695. Harvard reports a median of 685.

Slide 3
Grade point averages run from 3.6 at Chicago Booth and Columbia to 3.8 at Stanford. Harvard's average is 3.77.

Slide 4
Stanford's class averages 5.2 years of work experience, and Kellogg's 5.1. At Harvard, the middle 80 percent had 3 to 7 years.

Slide 5
Harvard, Wharton and Columbia ask for 3 essays, and Stanford for 2. Wharton, Chicago Booth and Columbia now ask for 1 recommendation, down from 2.

Slide 6
For the Class of 2027, Stanford admitted 6.8 percent of applicants and Harvard 11.2 percent. Kellogg admitted 28.1 percent.
