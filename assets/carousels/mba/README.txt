MBA carousel: what each file is for

The post is seven slides, 1080 x 1350 each:
  1  the question, with the seven school marks (moving)
  2  GMAT Focus scores by school
  3  GPAs by school
  4  years of work experience by school
  5  essays and recommendations each school asks for
  6  acceptance rates, Class of 2027
  7  Leland (moving; black, the end slide every post shares)

carousel.pdf       All seven slides, one per page. Slides 1 and 7 are their final frames.
slide-1.mp4        Slide 1 as a looping video with music (about 8 seconds).
slide-1.gif        The same loop as a GIF (no sound), for places that do not play video inline.
slide-1.png        Slide 1's final frame, for platforms that need an image.
slide-2.png to slide-6.png   The five still slides.
slide-7.mp4        The Leland slide as a short video with music (logo animation, then the line).
slide-7.png        The Leland slide's final frame.
slide-2-table.png, slide-3-table.png, slide-4-table.png, slide-6-table.png
                   The same figures as slides 2, 3, 4 and 6, as plain tables (the "Table" side of the Graph / Table switch
                   in the phone demo). Use either version of a slide.
all.png            All seven side by side, for a quick look.
alt/slide-2-with-booth.png, alt/slide-4-with-booth.png
                   Slides 2 and 4 with Chicago Booth included (see "Before posting publicly").
narration.txt      What the narrator says on each slide in the interactive version.

Where to post what
Instagram   slide-1.mp4 first, then slide-2.png to slide-6.png, then slide-7.mp4 (or slide-7.png).
            Trending music is added inside Instagram when posting; mute the built-in track there if you use one.
LinkedIn    carousel.pdf as a document post.
Slack       Drop in slide-1.gif or slide-1.mp4, plus carousel.pdf.

The interactive version (index.html, online or in the full folder, not in this zip) is swipeable, has a sound control,
and a narrator. The narrator there uses the device's own speech voice, which differs from device to device and is not in
the MP4. A posted video with narration needs a recorded or licensed voice.

Music
"Community outcomes", from Leland's own film music (assets/audio/community-outcomes-v4-preview.mp3).

Data source
Every number is from docs/research/viral-mba.md (researched 25 Nov 2026).
Slides 2 to 4: each school's class profile, Class of 2028 (Columbia: Class of 2027); Wharton and MIT Sloan as reported by Clear Admit.
Slide 5: GradPilot (checked 24 Sep 2026) and Poets&Quants, 2026-27 application cycle. Secondary sources.
Slide 6: Poets&Quants, using U.S. News data, Class of 2027 (entered autumn 2025).

Before posting publicly
Slide 7's statistic ("Leland MBA customers are admitted to M7 programs at 3x the national average") is Leland's own
internal figure and needs sign-off before it is posted publicly.
Chicago Booth is left off slides 2 and 4. The research notes ask for Booth's test-score lines and its "6 years" of work
experience to be re-read on the school's page first. Once confirmed (GMAT Focus median 675, average 6 years), use the
two files in alt/ in place of slide-2.png and slide-4.png, or delete the two "booth" lines under "hold" in src/facts.json
and rebuild.
MIT Sloan's essay count is not shown on slide 5 because the two sources describe its pieces differently.
Slide 5's requirements are from secondary sources; confirm on each school's site.
The headlines on slides 2 to 6 are plain factual labels and can be replaced. Slide 1's line is Michael's wording.
The school marks are the parent universities' marks, used to identify each school.
