Acceptance-rate carousel: what each file is for

The post is seven slides, 1080 x 1350 each:
  1  acceptance rates over time, fall 2014 to fall 2023 (moving)
  2  the fall 2023 ranking
  3  Northeastern
  4  applications, fall 2020 and fall 2021
  5  five schools, 2014 and 2023
  6  applied and admitted, fall 2023
  7  Leland (moving; black, the end slide every post shares)

slide-1.mp4        Slide 1 as a looping video with music: the chart over time.
slide-1.gif        The same loop as a GIF (no sound).
slide-1.png        Slide 1's final frame (fall 2023).

                   Graph / Table switch in the phone demo. Use either.

Data source
NCES IPEDS Admissions via the Urban Institute Education Data Portal: first-time degree-seeking undergraduate applicants
and admits, fall of each year. Acceptance rate = admitted / applied. The ranking covers every US institution reporting at
least 10,000 applicants and an entering class of at least 1,000 that fall (data/lists/ipeds-acceptance.json).
On the charts of slides 1, 3 and 5 the rate axis runs upside down (0% at the top), so a school that gets harder to get
into climbs; each of those charts says so on its axis.

Before posting publicly
Slide 7's statistic ("Leland MBA customers are admitted to M7 programs at 3x the national average") is Leland's own
internal figure and needs sign-off before it is posted publicly.
The headlines on slides 1 to 6 are plain factual labels and can be replaced.
The school marks are each school's own mark, used to identify it.
