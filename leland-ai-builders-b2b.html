› FAQ

Common questions from leaders.

01Who is this for?+
This program was created to help leaders from C-suite execs to mid-level managers move their teams and orgs from "we use AI" to "WE BUILD WITH AI." Programs can support teams from 5 seats to full Fortune 500 teams.
02How is this different from a typical AI training course?+
Most courses transfer information. We transfer capability. Every session ends with a deliverable your team uses at work the next day. Automations, agents, internal tools. Not slides.
03How do the 5 levels fit together?+
Each level is a standalone 3-week course you can take on its own. They also build on each other: Level 1 covers core AI fluency, Level 2 builds agents, Level 3 makes them autonomous, Level 4 orchestrates them at scale, Level 5 ships production systems. We'll help you place each team member at the right entry point. The full 5-Level system is meant to help take your knowledge workers from deliverable specialists to system engineers.
04Do team members need coding experience?+
No. The series is designed for knowledge workers, not engineers. Levels 4 and 5 go deep enough to challenge experienced builders, but Levels 1–3 prioritize practical application over code.
05What does it cost for a team?+
Pricing depends on team size, number of levels, and timeline. Teams of 5+ get volume pricing, a dedicated account manager, and custom-workshop options. We'll give you a clear range on the consult once we understand your team size and goals.
06How fast can we get started?+
New cohorts start regularly, typically every 4 weeks, so most teams kick off within two weeks of the consult. Your team ships their first automation in week one of Level 1.
07What if AI tools change after the course?+
Curriculum is updated continuously, and every seat includes 1 year of access to all subsequent cohorts, so your team always learns at the frontier.
08What if our company doesn't let us connect AI to internal systems?+
A core skill we teach is how to build effective AI workflows without connecting to your live data. Your team will still ship real automations within whatever constraints your IT policy sets. MCP connectors are taught as an optional capability, not a requirement.
