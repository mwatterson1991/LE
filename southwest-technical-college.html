No. The day is built for people at every level, and we shape it around who is in the room.

No. You pay the full amount first. Custom Fit can award up to 40%, but each business must qualify and the amount may be less depending on your application and available funds.

Why is there a call before I sign up?

So we can answer your questions, explain what Custom Fit offers, and set up the day for the kinds of people joining us.

Will there be another session?

To be determined. If this date doesn't work for you, please let us know and we will keep you updated on future cohorts.
