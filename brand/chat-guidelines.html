Chat interface
guidelines

Logo

  • 1Both bars paint down together into the //, then a sweep turns them into the curves.
  • 2The mark holds, then pulls back into three dots that spin.
  • 3The dots split into the cube. Each dot turns sky blue as its slice locks in.
  • 4Then the tricks: a turn around the centre, a flatten face-on, a tumble. Then back to three dots.
  • 5On photos the whole mark is white.

Orb

  • 1At rest it's seven dots, flat and still.
  • 2While it thinks, it turns like a Rubik's cube. The moving slice turns sky blue.
  • 3It never travels. It vanishes, then reappears beside the line being spoken.
  • 4Only the slices turn while it thinks. The cube itself never spins at the same time.
  • 5On photos it's white and never turns blue.
  • 6Keep it small. 24 to 64px in the interface.

Voice

  • 1Words arrive at speaking pace. Commas breathe, full stops land.
  • 2The important word gets a beat before it.
  • 3The thinking line names your last answer.

Answers

  • 1A choice stays as you made it. The widget locks and the chat carries on below.
  • 2Nothing is rewritten into a line of text.
  • 3Only typed answers become a grey bubble.
  • 4The input lights up the moment a typed question starts.
  • 5Tap any earlier answer to change it.
  • 6Reading text is spoken too, line by line, never dropped in all at once.

Icons

  • 1Logos for tools. Marks for who does the work.
  • 2If every option would get the same icon, none get one.
  • 3Sentences don't get icons. Icons are plain marks, never tinted tiles.

  • 3Brand photography is nature in motion: clouds, trees, clay, wheat, water, flowers. Grainy, blurred, never people.

  • 3No icon, no subcopy. Small white dots. Hover Continue and they become an arrow.
  • 4On Continue a prismatic wave rolls out from the button and carries the whole screen away to the chat.

Motion

  • 1Nothing above you ever moves.
  • 2The page scrolls only when you'd otherwise miss something.
  • 3When the question changes, it slides in, so you notice.
  • 4Chrome shimmer only on send and the step that's running.
  • 5No visible scrollbars. Nothing reserves space on the right.

Ten rules

  1. 1Show intelligence through motion, not more interface.
  2. 2Every element needs a reason to be there.
  3. 3A heading stands alone.
  4. 4No boxes, rules or dot grids around content.
  5. 5Blue means selected or right. Nothing else.
  6. 6Buttons are flat black, on the right.
  7. 7Answers stay as you gave them.
  8. 8Nothing shifts after you act.
  9. 9Speak like a person, never as "I".
  10. 10The brand lives in the moments between sections.
